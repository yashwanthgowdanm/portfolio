About

I'm a robotics engineer with an MS in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from Arizona State University (May 2026, 4.0 in my final semester) and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering. My focus is manipulation and perception on real hardware.

Fig. P-01 · 2026
P-01 · Manipulation + robot learning · ASU — NICE Lab, Prof. Zhe Xu

Autonomous Bottle Grasping on Stretch 3 with OpenVLA Data Collection

A fully autonomous grasp pipeline on a Hello Robot Stretch 3: YOLO11 finds the bottle in the D405 wrist camera, depth gives its distance, and the robot centers its base and lift in a coarse-to-fine macro and micro scan before extending, grasping and lifting. Every run is logged at 10 Hz as synchronized images, joint states and a language instruction, formatted for training the OpenVLA vision-language-action model.

StackPython · YOLO11 · Intel RealSense D405 · stretch_body · OpenCV · NumPy
ResultAutonomous grasp · 10 Hz OpenVLA-format episodes

Fig. P-02 · 2026
P-02 · Manipulation + perception · ASU — NICE Lab, Prof. Zhe Xu

Autonomous Hanger Pickup and Rack Placement on Stretch 3

The robot finds an orange hanger with HSV color segmentation, deprojects the grasp pixel to a 3D point using depth and camera intrinsics, and aligns its base, arm and lift to it. A guarded descent watches lift motor current to detect contact for a soft touchdown before grasping. The hanger is then placed on a garment rack with a scripted, hard-coded trajectory.

StackPython · OpenCV (HSV) · Intel RealSense D405 · stretch_body · NumPy
ResultAutonomous pickup · current-based contact detection · scripted rack placement
