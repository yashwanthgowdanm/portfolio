About

Sheet 01 / 07

I'm a robotics engineer with an MS in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from Arizona State University (May 2026). My focus is taking systems from simulation to physical deployment: ROS2, SLAM, Nav2, control systems, multi-sensor fusion and dexterous manipulation.

At Prof. Zhe Xu's Robotics Lab I build ROS2 control for the TetherIA Aero Hand, a 7-DOF tendon-driven hand integrated with a Stretch3 mobile manipulator. My projects span autonomous patrol, swarm robotics, control design and ML-based fault detection.

Alongside robotics I bring hands-on cybersecurity and GRC experience (ISO 27001 governance, audits, risk assessment), which matters as robots and industrial automation become more connected. I'm looking for full-time roles in robotics, autonomy, controls, automation and systems engineering, plus selected security/GRC roles.
